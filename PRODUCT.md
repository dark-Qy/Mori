# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use Apple Watch throughout the day and want a gentle, low-pressure companion rather
than a dense health dashboard. They meet Mori in short Watch interactions and use the iPhone
companion for history, privacy, collections, and reflective weekly review.

## Product Purpose

Watch Companion turns bounded health context, small controllable actions, story events, and trusted
social signals into a sustainable relationship with a virtual pet. Success means Mori feels
personally continuous across days, weekly reflection helps the person notice their own patterns
without diagnosis or blame, and the complete core experience remains useful without AI or network
availability.

## Brand Personality

Gentle, intimate, quietly adventurous. The product should feel like opening a well-kept illustrated
field journal with a familiar companion: warm enough to invite reflection, restrained enough to
keep health context trustworthy, and playful only where Mori or the shared world earns it.

## Anti-references

- A health-score dashboard, streak enforcer, productivity leaderboard, or medical monitor.
- A generic card-grid wellness app with decorative AI copy disconnected from real user actions.
- A phone-first virtual-pet game that duplicates the Watch experience or requires constant input.
- A generated storybook that changes Mori's identity, invents health facts, or hides uncertainty.
- Guilt, punishment, urgency, or pet harm caused by missing data or health outcomes.

## Design Principles

1. Mori is the interface: lead with one meaningful shared moment, then disclose evidence and detail.
2. Facts stay deterministic: rules and recorded events own observations, rewards, memory authority,
   and safety; Mock weekly reports use reviewed local copy and build-time Codex image2 assets.
3. Every memory changes something useful: it should support continuity, reduce repetition, or make a
   later event visibly more personal.
4. Reflection stays humane: compare only with the person's own usable history, show uncertainty,
   and suggest only controllable, low-pressure experiments.
5. Watch is for presence; iPhone is for reflection and control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, 44-point touch targets, high-contrast semantic colors,
Reduce Motion alternatives, and layouts that reflow at accessibility text sizes. Missing, stale,
partial, or unavailable health data must remain neutral. The build-time Mock illustration never
carries essential text or facts; every weekly memory remains understandable from deterministic copy
and an accessible description.
